# Halvorsen Labs Employee Benefits

Halvorsen Labs covers 100 percent of health, dental and vision insurance premiums for employees, and 80 percent for
dependents. Coverage begins on the first day of employment.

Every employee receives a wellness stipend of 50 dollars per month that can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes
or mental health apps. Unused stipend does not roll over.

Employees also receive an annual learning budget of 1,500 dollars for books, courses and conference tickets. Conference
travel paid from the learning budget still follows the business travel policy.

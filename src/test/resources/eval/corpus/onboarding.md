# Halvorsen Labs New Hire Onboarding

New hires receive their laptop by courier two business days before their start date. Orientation takes place on your
first Monday at 10:00 in the Fjord room, or over video for remote employees.

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first 90 days. The buddy is the first
point of contact for questions about tools, culture and where to find things.

During the first week, new hires agree on a 30-60-90 day plan with their manager. Access to production systems is only
granted after completing the security training module, which must be finished within the first 10 days.

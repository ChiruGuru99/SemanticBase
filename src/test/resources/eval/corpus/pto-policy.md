# Halvorsen Labs Paid Time Off Policy

Every full-time employee at Halvorsen Labs receives 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued monthly.
Unused days can be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days; anything above that expires on January 1.

Time off is requested through the Tempo portal. Requests longer than three consecutive days must be submitted at least
two weeks in advance and approved by your direct manager.

Halvorsen Labs offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents, regardless of gender, available within
the first year after a birth or adoption. Parental leave does not count against the 25 PTO days.

Sick days are tracked separately and are unlimited, but absences longer than five working days require a doctor's note.

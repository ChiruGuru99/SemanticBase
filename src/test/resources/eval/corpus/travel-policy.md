# Halvorsen Labs Business Travel Policy

All business travel must be booked through the Wayfarer booking tool so that the travel team can track employees during
emergencies. Bookings made outside Wayfarer are not reimbursed.

Flights shorter than six hours must be booked in economy class. Flights of six hours or longer may be booked in premium
economy or business class. Hotel stays are capped at 250 dollars per night in most cities and 350 dollars per night in
designated high-cost cities such as London, Tokyo and New York.

Trips must be booked at least 14 days before departure where possible. Rental cars require prior approval from your
manager, and ride-share is preferred for distances under 30 miles.

# Halvorsen Labs Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses. Meals while working away from the office are capped at
75 dollars per day, including tips. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

An itemized receipt is required for any single expense over 25 dollars. Expense reports must be submitted in the
Ledgerly app within 30 days of the purchase; reports filed later than that are rejected automatically.

Approval thresholds: expenses under 1,000 dollars are approved by your direct manager. Anything at or above 1,000 dollars
also needs sign-off from a vice president. Approved reimbursements are paid out with the next payroll run.

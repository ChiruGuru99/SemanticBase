# Halvorsen Labs Information Security Policy

Passwords for company systems must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they are
changed only when a compromise is suspected.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every company account and must use a hardware security key issued by IT.
SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor.

All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Company data
may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.

Suspected security incidents, including lost devices and phishing emails that were clicked, must be reported to the
security team within 1 hour of discovery through the #sec-incidents channel.
